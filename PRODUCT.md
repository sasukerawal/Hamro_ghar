# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Dual-sided marketplace: renters/room-seekers searching for a home or room across Nepal, and property owners/landlords listing homes or hostel spaces and managing inquiries. Seekers arrive wary of broker scams and fake listings; owners arrive wanting a fast, low-friction way to post and manage listings. Neither side is subordinate to the other — the homepage, search, and listing flows serve seekers; the posting wizard, membership dashboard, and profile serve owners.

## Product Purpose

HamroGhar helps people find and list real rental homes and hostel rooms across Nepal without brokers or middlemen. It exists to replace scam-prone, broker-mediated rental search with direct, verified, owner-to-seeker connections. Success is a seeker finding a real, trustworthy listing quickly and reaching out directly, or an owner getting their listing live and managed with minimal friction.

## Brand Personality

**Revised 2026-07-03 (third pass, same day): warmer re-tint of the premium positioning**, superseding the deep-navy-and-gold description below (that revision's positioning — polished, elevated craft, professionally-run — is unchanged; what was cold is now warm). The premium pivot itself stands: architectural beauty, elevated craft, and reliability over plainspoken utility. What changed is temperature, not ambition — navy read as boardroom-formal on review, so the ink and accent hues shifted to a warm espresso ink and a Kathmandu-brick terracotta. Still a well-funded, professionally designed property platform; just one that feels inviting rather than corporate-cold.

*Prior personality (superseded twice now):* originally trustworthy/local/no-nonsense/plainspoken/deliberately-not-flashy; then revised same-day to premium/aspirational with a navy-and-gold system. Both kept here for history.

## Anti-references

Generic AI SaaS template patterns still apply, unaffected by any pivot so far: cream/sand near-white PAGE-CANVAS backgrounds, gradient text, tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows above every section, identical icon+heading card grids, glassmorphism used decoratively/by default, the hero-metric-template cliché. Avoid cluttered ad-heavy classifieds-site density.

*Reversed by the 2026-07-03 premium pivot* (no longer anti-references): "stiff corporate real-estate polish" and "flashy funded startup" — premium/architectural/aspirational is the deliberate target. Avoid instead: looking cheap, discount, or amateur; generic stock-photo real-estate marketing with no local specificity; terracotta used decoratively across the whole surface (it stays a rare, restrained accent — verification, featured/premium markers, primary CTAs — not a wash-everything treatment, since restraint is what keeps it premium rather than gaudy); and — specific to this warm re-tint — reaching for a literal pale-cream page background as the "obvious" way to add warmth, which would just trade the navy+gold cliché for the more common cream+terracotta+serif one. Warmth here is a color-temperature choice in the ink/accent hues, not a canvas tint.

## Design Principles

- Elevated craft as the trust signal — polish and attention to detail communicate reliability now, alongside (not instead of) verification badges and real reviews.
- Serve both sides of the marketplace equally — seekers and owners each get a clear, undiluted path to their own task.
- Local specificity, premium execution — Nepal-rooted content (real districts, real listings) presented with the production values of a well-funded platform, not a translated global template.
- Real over generic — real photos, real listings, real reviews foregrounded over stock imagery or decorative filler, even within a more polished visual system.
- Restraint makes terracotta read as premium, not rustic — the accent color earns its "premium" reading precisely because it's rare (CTAs, verification, featured markers), not because it's everywhere.
- Warmth lives in hue temperature, not in the page canvas — an espresso ink and a fired-brick terracotta accent deliver "warm to the eyes" without tinting the background toward the generic cream-page default.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard WCAG 2.1 AA baseline: solid color contrast, full keyboard navigation, `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for all animation, readable text sizes. No additional known user-specific requirements beyond this baseline.
